About Kirkland

Kirkland is a vibrant lakeside city known for its waterfront parks, public art, tech presence, and close-knit neighborhoods.

History of Kirkland

1886

Founded by Peter Kirk to be the “Pittsburgh of the West.”

1905

Ferry access to Seattle accelerates growth.

1980s

Tech industry expansion transforms the city.

2011

Major annexation increases population.

Today

Kirkland thrives with culture, business, and lake recreation.
